# Work Log

**Your day, timed lap by lap.** A stopwatch work log for Android: start the clock, tap Lap when you switch tasks, and keep your streak, XP and level going.

**[Download WorkLog.apk](https://github.com/arunava-ctrl/work-log/releases/latest/download/WorkLog.apk)** — open it on your phone to install. The app tells you itself when a newer version is out.

[Privacy](https://arunava-ctrl.github.io/work-log/privacy.html): your log stays on your phone and, if you sign in, in a private folder of your own Google Drive. Nothing goes to any other server.
