Privacy

Work Log · last updated 24 October 2026

Work Log is a personal work diary. It has no server of its own, and no ads, analytics or tracking.

What's stored, and where

  • On your device: everything you enter (days, laps, tasks, notes, times, pictures, projects and settings) is saved inside the app.
  • Backups: on Android, automatic backup saves a file in Documents/WorkLog, and Android's own backup can keep a copy in your Google account. On iPhone, a backup is only made when you save one to Files.
  • Google sign-in (Android, optional): your log is saved as one file in the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive, so your devices can sync. Work Log asks only for that folder (drive.appdata) and your name, email address and profile picture, which show on Me. It can't see or change any other files in your Drive.
  • Update check (Android): once a day the app asks GitHub for the newest version number. No log data is sent; GitHub sees the visit like any website does.
  • Reminders, the live timer and widgets are made on your device and send nothing anywhere.

Who can see it

Only you. Data goes straight between your device and Google. The developer never receives it, and nothing is sold or shared.

Deleting your data

Use Me → Erase all data in the app. To remove the synced copy, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Work Log, and choose "Delete hidden app data". You can remove Work Log's access to your account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
