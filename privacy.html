Privacy

Work Log · last updated 2 October 2026

Work Log is a personal work diary. It has no server of its own and no analytics, ads or tracking.

What's stored, and where

  • On your device: everything you enter (days, tasks, notes, times, pictures, tags and settings) is saved in the app or in your browser.
  • Automatic backup: the Android app saves a backup file in your phone's Documents/WorkLog folder and, through Android's own backup, in your Google account. In a browser, a backup is saved only to a file you choose.
  • If you sign in with Google: your log is saved as one file in the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive, so your devices can sync. Work Log asks only for that app folder (drive.appdata) and your name, email address and profile picture, which are shown on your Profile. It can't see or change any other files in your Drive.

Who can see it

Only you. Your data goes directly between your device and Google's servers. The developer never receives it, and nothing is sold or shared.

Deleting your data

Use Profile → Erase all data in the app. To remove the synced copy, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Work Log, and choose "Delete hidden app data". You can remove Work Log's access to your account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
